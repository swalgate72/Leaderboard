Auto Adjustment
How it works
The idea

After each round, each player's handicap index is nudged up or down based on how their score compared to the round average. Shoot above average → index goes down (you're playing better). Shoot below average → index goes up (you need more shots).

The formula
delta = (mean − yourPoints) ÷ strength
newIndex = currentIndex + delta (rounded to 1 d.p.)
Worked example (strength = 3)
Round mean: 32 pts
Your score: 38 pts (6 above mean)
delta = (32 − 38) ÷ 3 = −2.0
If your index was 18.4, it becomes 16.4
Strength settings
Strong (2): 2 pts above mean → −1.0 index — fast response
Balanced (3): 3 pts above mean → −1.0 index — default
Gentle (4): 4 pts above mean → −1.0 index — slow drift
Important rules
  • Adjustments are calculated by the server after the organiser finalises each round.
  • A player must complete all 18 holes to count toward the mean (DNFs are excluded).
  • The mean requires at least 2 completed scores. If fewer complete, no adjustment is made.
  • If the organiser finalises with some DNFs, they'll be asked to confirm before adjustments run.
